# Privacy Policy for Rei Personal

Rei Personal is a private desktop application built and used by one person for personal use only. It is not offered to any other users.

**What it does:** It reads the balance of the owner's own bank account through the Enable Banking API, read-only, and displays it on the owner's own computer.

**What data is used:** Account balance information only.

**Where data is stored:** Only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to or stored on any server operated by the app, and no data is shared with third parties.

**Access:** The app cannot make payments or change anything on the account. The owner can revoke access at any time through the bank or Enable Banking.

**Changes:** This policy may be updated if the app changes.
